How to Choose the Right Battery for Your Home or business

  1. Calculate Your Load & Backup Needs: Determine backup hours needed, then size battery capacity accordingly.
  2. Decide Chemistry Based on Needs: Lithium for longevity and low maintenance, tubular lead-acid for cost-sensitive systems.
  3. Check Real Performance: Look how well a battery works in hot weather, and how it performs in real conditions in your area.
  4. Warranty, After-sales & Brand Strength: A strong warranty with good customer service reach is essential.
  5. Integration & Compatibility: Ensure your battery works with your inverter and system design, with scope for expansion if needed.
