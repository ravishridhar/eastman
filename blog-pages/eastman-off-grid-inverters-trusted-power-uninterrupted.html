FAQ’s

Q. What is an off-grid inverter and how does it differ from a grid-tied inverter?

A. An off-grid inverter is designed to operate independently of the utility grid, converting DC power from solar panels and batteries into AC power for local consumption. A grid-tied inverter, conversely, is designed to work in conjunction with the utility grid, often feeding excess solar power back into it. Off-grid inverters typically include a battery charger and can manage power flow between solar, battery, and load autonomously.
