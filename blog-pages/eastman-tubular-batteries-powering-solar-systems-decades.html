Why Eastman Tubular Batteries Are Integral to Solar Success

When it comes to solar energy storage, not all batteries are created equal. Solar systems demand batteries that can handle deep discharge cycles, operate reliably in diverse climates, and offer an exceptional lifespan. This is precisely where Eastman Tubular Batteries shine. Their core strength lies in their ability to endure frequent, deep discharges without compromising capacity or longevity, unlike conventional flat plate batteries, tubular batteries feature positive plates constructed with lead oxide paste filled into a woven bag of fine fibre, encased in a polymer tube. This unique design offers several distinct advantages critical for solar applications:

  • Enhanced Cycling Capability: Solar systems require batteries that can withstand frequent and deep discharges without significant capacity loss. Eastman's tubular batteries are designed for deep cycling, ensuring consistent power delivery even after prolonged periods of use, ensuring maximum energy utilization from your panels.
  • Extended Lifespan: The robust construction of tubular plates resists shedding of active material, which is a common cause of failure in other battery types. This structural integrity translates directly into an exceptionally longer service life, often extending well beyond ten years, significantly reducing replacement costs and environmental impact.
  • Robust Construction: Designed to withstand with harsh operating and weather conditions, making them ideal for the challenging environments often associated with solar installations.
  • Superior Charge Acceptance: Efficiently absorbing energy from solar panels is crucial. Eastman tubular batteries exhibit excellent charge acceptance, ensuring quicker and more complete charging cycles, even under varying sunlight conditions.
  • Minimal Maintenance: Engineered for convenience, these batteries require significantly less water topping up compared to their counterparts, making them a low-maintenance, high-performance choice for residential & commercial segments.

FAQ’s

Q: What are tubular batteries, and how do they differ from flat plate batteries?

A: Tubular batteries feature positive plates made from lead tubes filled with active material, offering a larger surface area and better structural integrity compared to flat plate batteries. This design makes them significantly more resistant to corrosion and shedding, leading to a much longer lifespan and superior deep-cycling performance, which is ideal for solar applications.

Q: Why are Eastman Tubular Batteries recommended for solar systems?

A: Eastman Tubular Batteries are specifically engineered for solar applications due to their exceptional deep-cycle capability, high charge acceptance, and low self-discharge rate. Their robust construction ensures durability and consistent performance even in challenging environmental conditions, providing reliable power backup for decades.

Q: How long can I expect Eastman Tubular Batteries to last in a solar setup? 

A: With proper installation, maintenance, and usage, Eastman Tubular Batteries are designed to offer an exceptionally long service life, often extending for many decades. Their superior design and quality materials contribute to their remarkable longevity, making them a wise long-term investment for solar power systems.

Q: Do Eastman Tubular Batteries require a lot of maintenance? 

A: While tubular batteries generally require less maintenance than conventional batteries, it's still good practice to periodically check electrolyte levels (for flooded types) and keep the terminals clean to ensure optimal performance and longevity. Eastman batteries are designed to be low maintenance, simplifying your solar system management.
