Eastman’s Journey from E‑Rickshaw Batteries to One Stop Solar Rooftop Solutions

Oct, 31 2025

From powering India’s last-mile mobility to driving the rooftop solar revolution, Eastman’s trajectory is nothing short of inspiring. What began as a battery-maker for e-rickshaws has evolved into a full-spectrum clean-energy solutions provider now offering solar panels, inverters, batteries and turnkey rooftop systems. In this blog, we trace Eastman’s journey, spotlight its pivot into solar rooftop solutions and battery landscapes.

From E‑Rickshaw traction to scaling manufacturing 
 
Eastman established strong roots in the e‑rickshaw battery domain, building battery packs used in traction applications lead‑acid-based technology suited for durability and long‑haul driving cycles. It has produced and sold over millions of batteries for e‑rickshaws, marking a major milestone that demonstrates its scale, market acceptance, and credibility in the electric mobility domain. 
 
The manufacturing base is substantial: Eastman runs lead‑acid battery manufacturing facilities across multiple plants. As of now, it has the capacity to produce millions of battery units per year for various applications. With partnerships with OEMs in automotive and energy storage sectors, it built a strong distribution / channel network, enabling scale in both mobility and stationary applications.

Evolution into integrated solar & energy storage solutions 
 
Recognising the synergies between battery technology and renewable energy, Eastman expanded into solar batteries, solar inverters, hybrid systems and full solar solutions. Their product portfolio now includes solar batteries (for off‑grid / hybrid systems), rooftop solar inverters, hybrid inverters + storage solutions, etc. 
 
Their hybrid inverters allow integration of solar panels + battery storage + grid, enabling households or businesses to make use of stored solar energy, reduce grid dependency, and manage energy cost & reliability. 
 
They have expanded product lines massively, launching new inverter battery models across different capacities, catering to residential, commercial, industrial backup and solar storage applications. On the solar front, they offer not just batteries but the whole solar + inverter + storage stack, positioning themselves as a one‑stop shop for residential or commercial solar installations. 
 
Driving forward: vision, innovation & market positioning 
 
Eastman is not stopping at battery + inverter. It’s ready for advanced chemistry, battery packs for solar storage, and mobility applications. 
 
They are investing strongly in R&D, improving battery chemistries to lithium and advanced storage solutions suited for hybrid solar + storage systems. By offering integrated solar + hybrid inverter + battery combos, Eastman is enabling homes, businesses and rural communities to adopt clean energy in a simpler, more accessible way, bridging the gap between mobility and stationary power solutions. 
 
Conclusion: A brand transformed 
 
The journey of Eastman is a powerful example of evolution in action: from helping power last‑mile e‑rickshaws on Indian streets, to becoming a full‑fledged provider of solar energy solutions. The credibility built in mobility has laid the foundation for a broader role in the renewable energy sector. 
 
Today, Eastman stands as a trusted brand that offers complete solutions — batteries, inverters, solar storage, and hybrid systems. Consumers no longer need to look elsewhere; Eastman is positioning itself as a go‑to, one‑stop shop for clean energy needs. 
 
For those looking to adopt solar + backup + renewable energy systems, Eastman offers a compelling mix of heritage, innovation, capacity, and integrated offerings that make it a top contender among solar and battery brands in India.
