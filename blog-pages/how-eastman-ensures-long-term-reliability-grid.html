Here are some Key facts about Grid-Tie & Hybrid Inverters: 

 

1. Grid-Tie Inverters- A grid-tie inverter connects your solar panels directly to the utility grid. Its primary function is to convert Direct Current (DC) from solar panels into Alternating Current (AC) compatible with your home appliances and the grid. 

  • Key Function: It synchronizes with the grid's voltage and frequency. 

  • Benefit: Excess energy generated can often be fed back into the grid, potentially earning your credits through net metering. 

  • Limitation: For safety reasons, standard grid-tie inverters automatically shut down during a power outage to prevent back-feeding electricity into lines that utility workers might be repairing. 

 

2. Hybrid Inverters- Hybrid inverters offer a more comprehensive solution, combining the capabilities of a grid-tie inverter with a battery inverter. This system manages power from solar panels, battery storage, and the grid simultaneously. 

  • Key Function: It can store excess solar energy in batteries for use during the evening or power outages. 

  • Benefit: Provides energy security and independence, ensuring critical appliances remain operational when the grid goes down. 

  • Relevance: For those prioritizing security and uninterrupted power, a hybrid system acts as a safeguard against grid instability.

Some Maintenance & Longevity Tips

While Eastman engineers' inverters are designed for durability, homeowners can further extend their investment lifespan through simple maintenance practices. 

1. Ensure Proper Ventilation: Do not block the airflow around the inverter. Ensure there is at least 6 to 12 inches of clearance around the unit. Do not stack boxes or other household items against it, as this traps heat and forces the cooling system to work harder. 

2. Regular Visual Inspections: Periodically inspect the inverter for any signs of physical damage or obstruction. Check the air intake vents for dust buildup. If dust is present, a gentle wipe with a dry cloth or using a soft brush can improve airflow significantly. 

3. Monitor Error Logs: Modern Eastman inverters come with monitoring capabilities. Occasional checks of the system status via the display or associated app can help you catch minor warnings before they become significant issues. If you notice persistent error codes, contact our support team for personalized assistance.
