Top 10 Battery Manufacturers in India

Sep, 23 2025

India’s energy sector is evolving rapidly witnessing remarkable growth, with rising demand for inverter batteries, lithium-ion batteries, EV solutions, and solar storage. People are actively searching for the best battery brands in India, and this is pushing manufacturers to innovate faster than ever.

As the market expands, competition among manufacturers is getting tougher, and only those brands that focus on quality, innovation, and strong service support are making it to the top. In this blog, we bring you the Top 10 Battery Manufacturers in India 2025 that are shaping the country’s energy future.

1. Eastman Auto & Power Ltd.

With over 20 years of experience, Eastman is the “best battery manufacturer in India (2025)” with a wide portfolio ranging from inverter batteries to advanced lithium-ion and EV battery packs. Trusted in more than 50 countries, Eastman has become the “top choice for homes, industries, and renewable energy storage”.

Why Eastman ranks one of the top battery manufacturers in India:

  • Offers best inverter batteries in India with long backup and durability
  • Global Reach: Serving in over 50 countries, working with OEMs etc.
  • Advanced R&D and large-scale production capacity
  • Reliable service support and nationwide presence

2. Exide Industries Ltd.

Exide remains one of the oldest battery companies in India, best known for automotive, inverter, and industrial batteries. With decades of trust, advanced technology, catering to a wide range of applications.

3. Su-Kam Power Systems

Su-Kam Known for its innovative technology and reliable backup products, its products continue to support a wide customer base, ranging from individual to commercial. The brand is especially recognized for its tubular inverter batteries and UPS systems, making it a strong name.

4. Amara Raja Batteries Ltd.

Amara Raja has built a strong presence in the Indian battery industry. Popular for its Amaron brand, it holds a significant presence across automotive and inverter categories. The company is now investing heavily in lithium-ion and EV segments.

5. Luminous Power Technologies

Backed by over three decades of expertise, Luminous has become a leading name in the inverter battery market in India. Known for reliable backup solutions and affordable products, it has now also expanded into lithium-ion technology for homes and offices.

6. Okaya Power Pvt. Ltd.

Okaya, part of the 29-year-old Microtek Group, has been delivering power solutions and is widely regarded as one of the brands focused on durable products and innovation, with strong offerings in solar, inverter, and EV segments.

7. HBL Power Systems Ltd.

The company is a trusted leader in industrial, telecom, railway, and defense segments, with nearly five decades of experience in designing and manufacturing specialized batteries. It is known for delivering high-performance and mission-critical energy solutions.

8. Southern Batteries Pvt. Ltd.

It is a trusted Indian manufacturer specializing in tubular, automotive, and industrial batteries. The company has decades of expertise in inverter batteries and traction batteries used in railways and forklifts. Southern Batteries is known for its durability and performance.

9. Panasonic Energy India

Panasonic Energy India is a subsidiary of Panasonic Corporation, Japan. It is a leading manufacturer of dry cells and rechargeable batteries in India. Known for Japanese quality standards, the company provides safe, eco-friendly, and durable energy solutions.

10. Waaree ESS

Part of the Waaree Group founded in 1989, Waaree ESS is an emerging name in the lithium-ion battery space. With expertise in solar, renewable energy storage & innovation-driven approach the company is rapidly building its presence in India’s clean energy ecosystem.

Conclusion

The government’s strong push for electric mobility, renewable energy, and sustainable storage solutions is creating immense opportunities for India’s battery industry. With supportive policies, rising EV adoption, and the growing need for reliable backup power, the future looks highly promising for the top 10 battery manufacturers in India. Their role will be critical in driving India’s energy transition, enabling clean mobility, and ensuring energy independence.

Among them, Eastman Auto & Power Ltd. stands out as one the strongest battery player - combining innovation, durability, and global expertise to meet the country’s ambitious energy goals.
