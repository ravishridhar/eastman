Understanding Solar Panel Warranty, Degradation & Long-Term Output

Feb, 19 2026

As rooftop solar adoption accelerates across India, more homeowners and businesses are evaluating not just the price of solar panels, but also their long-term performance and reliability. 

While solar panels are marketed with 25-year warranties, many buyers do not fully understand: 

A. What does the warranty actually cover? 

B. How do solar panels degrade over time? 

C. What happens to solar panel output after 10 or 20 years? 

D. Does degradation affect ROI?

For Indian consumers investing in rooftop solar systems, understanding solar panel warranty in India is essential for making a future-ready decision. 

What is a Solar Panel Warranty?

A solar panel warranty is the manufacturer’s assurance regarding: Product quality and Performance over time.

There are typically two types of warranties: 

1. Product Warranty (Physical Warranty) 

This covers: 

A. Manufacturing defects 

B. Material failures 

C. Workmanship issues 

Most manufacturers offer a 10- to 15-year product warranty, ensuring protection against early panel failure. 

2. Performance Warranty (Power Output Warranty) 

This guarantees: 

A. Minimum power output over time 

B. Performance stability across 25 years 

Most panels guarantee: 

a. Around 90% output after 10–12 years 

b. Around 80–85% output after 25 years 

This is known as the solar panel performance warranty. 

What is Solar Panel Degradation?

All solar panels gradually lose efficiency over time. This process is called the solar panel degradation rate. 

Degradation occurs due to: 

  1. UV exposure

  2. Thermal cycling

  3. Environmental stress

  4. Micro-cracks

  5. Moisture exposure 

A typical annual degradation rate for high-quality solar panels is: 0.4% to 0.7% per year 

A lower degradation rate means better long-term output. 

How does Solar Panel Output change over 25 Years?

Let’s consider a simple example. If you install a 5kWrooftop solar system: 

A. Year 1 output = 100%

B. After 10 years (0.5% annual degradation) = ~95% 

C. After 25 years = ~87–88% 

This means your solar panel output over 25 years remains largely stable if high-quality panels are used. 

Panels with higher degradation rates may drop below 80% before 20 years, affecting long-term savings. 

Solar Panel Lifespan in India: What to Expect

In Indian conditions, panels are exposed to: 

  1. High heat (45°C+)

  2. Dust and pollution

  3. Humidity in coastal areas

  4. Heavy rainfall 

High-quality panels designed for the Indian climate typically offer: 

  1. 25+ years lifespan

  2. Strong encapsulation

  3. Anti-PID protection

  4. Robust frame construction 

When evaluating solar panel reliability in India, climate compatibility is crucial. 

Linear Warranty vs Step Warranty

Modern solar panels usually offer a linear performance warranty instead of a step warranty. 

Step Warranty 

Performance drops sharply after a fixed period. 

Linear Warranty 

Performance decreases gradually year by year at a defined degradation rate. 

For example: 

A. Maximum 2% drop in the first year 

B. 0.45% annual degradation thereafter 

A linear warranty provides more predictable long-term output. 

Why Degradation Matters for Solar ROI in India?

Many buyers focus only on: 

  1. Initial system cost

  2. Subsidy

  3. Payback period 

However, degradation affects: 

  1. Total lifetime energy generation

  2. Long-term savings

  3. Return on investment 

A panel with 0.5% annual degradation will generate significantly more electricity over 25 years compared to one degrading at 0.8%. This directly impacts your solar ROI in India.

How to evaluate Solar Panel Warranty before Buying

Before selecting a rooftop solar system, check: 

  1. Product warranty duration

  2. Performance warranty period (usually 25 years)

  3. Guaranteed power output percentage

  4. Annual degradation rate

  5. Brand credibility

  6. Service support network 

Choosing the best solar panels with a long warranty in India ensures financial stability over decades.

Common Misconceptions about Solar Panel Warranty

“A 25-year warranty means free replacement anytime.” 

Not necessarily. Warranty terms apply to defined performance thresholds and manufacturing defects. 

“Panels stop working after 25 years.” 

No. Panels may continue generating electricity beyond 25 years, but at reduced output. 

“All panels degrade at the same rate.” 

No. High-quality panels degrade more slowly, ensuring better long-term solar panel efficiency.

FAQ’s

Q1. What is the typical solar panel warranty in India? 

Most solar panels offer a 10–15-year product warranty and a 25-year performance warranty. 

Q2. What is the solar panel degradation rate? 

It is the percentage of efficiency loss a solar panel experiences each year. High-quality panels typically degrade at 0.4%–0.7% annually. 

Q3. How much output will solar panels produce after 25 years? 

Most panels guarantee 80–85% of their original output under a linear performance warranty after 25 years. 

Q4. What is the difference between a product warranty and a performance warranty? 

The product warranty covers manufacturing defects, while the performance warranty guarantees minimum power output over time. 

Q5. Do solar panels stop working after 25 years? 

No. Panels continue generating electricity beyond 25 years, but at slightly reduced efficiency. 

Q6. Does lower degradation improve solar ROI? 

Yes. Lower degradation ensures higher electricity generation over time, improving overall return on investment.
