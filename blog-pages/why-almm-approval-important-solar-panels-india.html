Why ALMM Approval Is Important for Solar Panels in India

Feb, 18 2026

As solar energy adoption expands rapidly across India, quality assurance and regulatory compliance have become increasingly important. Whether for residential rooftop systems, commercial installations, or industrial solar plants, buyers now evaluate not only price and efficiency but also certification and government approval. 

One term that has gained significant importance in recent years is ALMM approval for solar panels. 

But what exactly is ALMM? Why is it mandatory for certain projects? And how does it impact homeowners and businesses investing in solar energy? 

Understanding ALMM approval is essential for anyone planning to install a rooftop solar system in India.

What is ALMM Solar?

ALMM stands for an Approved List of Models and Manufacturers. 

It is a list issued by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) that includes solar panel manufacturers and specific panel models that meet defined quality and manufacturing standards. 

In simple terms, if a solar panel is ALMM approved, it means it is recognized by the Government of India for use in approved solar projects.

Why was ALMM introduced in India?

The Government of India introduced ALMM to: 

  1. Ensure high-quality solar installations

  2. Promote domestic solar manufacturing

  3. Reduce dependence on imported modules

  4. Protect consumers from substandard products

  5. Standardize solar panel quality standards 

Before ALMM, the Indian market experienced a large influx of low-cost imported panels, some of which lacked long-term durability and reliability.

ALMM approval ensures that only verified and compliant solar panel models are used in government-supported and many grid-connected projects.

Is ALMM approval Mandatory?

Yes, in many cases. ALMM-approved solar panels are mandatory for: 

  1. Government solar projects

  2. Rooftop solar systems installed under subsidy schemes

  3. Projects supported by MNRE

  4. Systems connected under specific DISCOM guidelines 

If you are applying for a subsidy under schemes such as PM Surya Ghar Yojana, using MNRE-approved solar panels is typically required. 

For private installations without subsidies, ALMM may not always be legally mandatory, but it is strongly recommended.

ALMM approval & Solar Panel Subsidy eligibility in India

One of the most important reasons for ALMM approval matters is subsidy eligibility. Under residential rooftop solar subsidy schemes: 

  1. Only ALMM-approved solar panels qualify

  2. Non-approved modules may disqualify for subsidy claims 

For homeowners planning a rooftop solar system in India, this directly impacts financial benefits. Installing non-compliant panels may result in: 

  1. Rejection of subsidy

  2. Delays in approvals

  3. Compliance issues during inspection 

Therefore, checking ALMM approval before installation is essential. 

Difference between ALMM & BIS Certification

Many buyers confuse ALMM with BIS certification. Here is the difference: 

BIS-Certified Solar Panels: Bureau of Indian Standards certification ensures: 

  1. Product safety

  2. Electrical standards compliance

  3. Testing under Indian conditions

ALMM-Approved Solar Panels: Approved List of Models & Manufacturers ensures: 

  1. Manufacturer approval by MNRE

  2. Compliance with domestic manufacturing policies

  3. Eligibility for government projects

  4. Quality validation beyond just testing 

In simple terms, BIS certification focuses on product standards, while ALMM approval focuses on approved manufacturers and models under government policy. 

Both are important for maintaining solar panel standards in India.

How ALMM Protects Consumers?

For homeowners and businesses investing in solar, reliability and long-term assurance matter more than short-term price savings. ALMM approval offers: 

  1. Verified manufacturing quality

  2. Reduced risk of low-grade imports

  3. Higher system reliability

  4. Better long-term warranty credibility

  5. Confidence in performance standards 

It ensures that your government-approved solar panel installation meets national guidelines.

Risks of Installing Non-ALMM Solar Panels: Installing non-approved modules may result in- 

  1. Ineligibility for solar subsidies

  2. Reduced the resale value of the property

  3. Questionable warranty enforcement

  4. Compliance issues with DISCOM

  5. Lower long-term reliability 

For residential and commercial buyers, this increases risk. A certified and compliant installation protects your investment.

Impact of ALMM On Domestic Solar Manufacturing

ALMM also supports India’s push for: 

  1. The Make in India initiative

  2. A self-reliant renewable energy sector

  3. Growth in domestic solar module manufacturing 

By prioritizing approved domestic manufacturers, ALMM strengthens the local solar industry and improves quality consistency. 

This policy plays a major role in achieving India’s renewable energy expansion targets.

How to check whether a Solar Panel is ALMM approved or not?

Before installation: 

  1. Ask your installer for the module model number.

  2. Verify it on the official MNRE ALMM list.

  3. Ensure that the specific model is listed (not just the brand).

  4. Confirm BIS certification as well. 

This simple verification ensures your system meets solar installation compliance standards in India.

FAQ’s

Q1. What is ALMM approval for solar panels? 

ALMM stands for Approved List of Models and Manufacturers issued by MNRE. It includes solar panel manufacturers and models approved for use in government-supported projects in India. 

Q2. Is ALMM approval mandatory in India? 

Yes, it is mandatory for government solar projects, and most rooftop solar systems are installed under subsidy schemes. 

Q3. Can I get solar subsidies without ALMM approved panels? 

No. Most government subsidy programs require the use of ALMM approved solar panels. 

Q4. What is the difference between BIS certification and ALMM approval? 

BIS certification ensures product safety and technical standards, while ALMM approval confirms that the manufacturer and model are approved by MNRE for eligible projects. 

Q5. How can I check if a solar panel is approved by ALMM? 

You can verify the manufacturer and specific model on the official MNRE ALMM list before installation. 

Q6. Why is ALMM important for rooftop solar systems in India? 

It ensures compliance with government policies, subsidy eligibility, and long-term reliability.
