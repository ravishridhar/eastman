What does 6000 Charge Cycles mean in a Lithium Battery?

A charge cycle refers to one complete discharge and recharge of a battery. For example: 

  1. Using 100% of battery capacity and recharging it = 1 cycle

  2. Using 50% today and 50% tomorrow before recharging = 1 full cycle 

If a battery is rated at 6000 charge cycles, it means it can be charged and discharged 6000 times before its capacity reduces to around 80% of its original level. 

This directly defines the lithium battery cycle life. For Indian solar users who rely on daily cycling, this number determines how many years the battery will last.

Depth of Discharge & Its Impact on Lithium Battery Cycle Life

Another critical factor is Depth of Discharge (DoD). Lithium batteries typically allow 80%–90% DoD safely. Lead-acid batteries typically allow only around 50% DoD 

This means lithium batteries provide: 

  1. More usable energy daily

  2. Less strain per cycle

  3. Better overall lithium battery lifespan in India 

When evaluating the best lithium battery for solar in India, always check both cycle life and DoD rating.

Role of BMS in achieving 6000 Cycles
