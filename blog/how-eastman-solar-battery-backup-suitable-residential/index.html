Lithium-ion vs Lead-Acid: Which Eastman Battery to Choose?

Lithium-ion:

  • Life Up to 6,000 cycles

  • Lightweight & compact

  • Higher efficiency

  • Ideal for hybrid commercial/residential use

  • Faster charging & deeper discharge

Tubular Lead-Acid:

  • Economical

  • Rugged & temperature-resistant

  • Reliable for moderate usage

  • Strong performance in off-grid setups

Recommendation: Choose Lithium-ion for long-term ROI and heavy daily use. Choose Tubular for budget-friendly and reliable backup needs.
