Understanding PM Surya Ghar Yojana 

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana aims to light up 1 crore households across India with rooftop solar panels, promising up to 300 units of free electricity every month. Launched with a significant investment, the scheme is designed to make solar energy accessible and affordable for every Indian family. 

Highlights that make this scheme truly revolutionary:

  1. Substantial Subsidies: The government provides direct subsidies, significantly reducing the upfront cost of solar panel installation. This financial incentive is a game changer, making solar energy a viable option for a wider demography.

  2. Low Electricity Bills: By generating your own power, you can dramatically cut down, or even eliminate, your monthly electricity expenses, leading to substantial long-term savings.

  3. Energy Independence: Break free from the volatility of grid power and contribute to a strong energy ecosystem.

  4. Environmental Balance: Embrace a cleaner lifestyle, reduce your carbon footprint, and play an active role in combating climate change.

This initiative is not merely an energy project it's a societal shift towards greater self-reliance and environmental responsibility. 

How Households are Embracing Solar Independence 

Since its inception, PM Surya Ghar Yojana has seen tremendous momentum. The simplified application process, accessible through a dedicated national portal, has streamlined the journey for homeowners. From metros to smaller towns, families are registering in droves, eager to harness the power of the sun. 

The impact is Visible as:

  1. Financial Liberation: Many households report significant reductions in their electricity bills, with some even achieving net-zero costs. This extra disposable income can be channeled into other household needs or savings.

  2. Increased Awareness: The scheme has ignited a national conversation around renewable energy and sustainable practices, fostering a generation more aware of their environmental impact.

  3. Grid expansion: Residential solar installations contribute decentralized power to the national grid, enhancing energy security, and reducing transmission losses. 

This progress is reflected in the growing demand for reliable solar panels, efficient solar inverters, and high-performance solar batteries. Homeowners are not just looking for a quick fix; they are investing in long-term energy solutions that promise consistent performance and durability.

Navigating Your Solar Journey 

Embarking on your residential solar project under PM Surya Ghar is simpler than you think. Here's a quick overview of the process:

  1. Register: Visit the official PM Surya Ghar portal and register with your state, district, electricity distribution company, and consumer account number.

  2. Apply: Log in, select "Rooftop Solar," and submit your application with necessary details.

  3. Vendor Selection & Installation: Choose a qualified vendor like Eastman, who will guide you through the technical feasibility and installation process. Our team ensures that your system is designed and installed to meet all scheme requirements and safety standards.

  4. Net Metering & Inspection: After installation, apply for net-metering. Your DISCOM will inspect the system and install a net meter, allowing you to export excess electricity to the grid.

  5. Subsidy Reimbursement: Once approved, the subsidy amount will be directly credited to your bank account.
