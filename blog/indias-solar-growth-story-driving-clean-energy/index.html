Government Initiatives Driving India’s Solar Revolution 
 
India’s solar success didn’t happen overnight it’s been powered by strong government vision and well-planned policies. The National Solar Mission, launched in 2010 under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), was the turning point that placed solar energy at the center of India’s renewable agenda. Since then, a series of ambitious initiatives have accelerated the country’s solar growth.  

Government Programs like PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana empower residential households to generate their own electricity with a subsidy varying from 40-60% for different setups. PM-KUSUM yojna enables farmers to generate their own electricity through solar pumps and even sell surplus power back to the grid. The Production Linked Incentive Scheme is boosting domestic manufacturing of solar panels, reducing dependence on imports, and promoting “Make in India” innovation. Together, these initiatives shape a future which has accelerated this growth, making solar energy more affordable and accessible to millions.
