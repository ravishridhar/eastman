Why Eastman Is India’s Trusted Energy Brand for Over Two Decades

Oct, 29 2025

In a rapidly evolving energy landscape, trust is earned through consistency, innovation, and a deep understanding of consumer needs. Over the last twenty years, Eastman Auto & Power has transformed from a local battery manufacturer into a pan‑India, global energy solutions provider. With footprints in solar inverters, energy storage batteries (Conventional Tubular and lithium‑ion), and last‑mile electric mobility solutions, Eastman has built a reputation not just for products, but for reliability, performance, and service excellence. 
 
From residential homes relying on backup inverters to commercial setups adopting rooftop solar + storage to e‑rickshaw fleets using lithium battery packs, the brand has stood the test of time earning trust through quality, scale, and an integrated approach covering generation, storage, and power conversion. 
Legacy of excellence: manufacturing, scale & innovation 
 
Decades of manufacturing leadership: 
Eastman Auto & Power was established in the early 2000s and has evolved into a leading power solutions provider in India. The company now operates multiple manufacturing facilities and has built capacity at scale, across its businesses. 
 
R&D, product portfolio, and technology integration: 
A robust research & development wing with more than 120+ engineers to drive continuous innovation in battery storage, power electronics, and solar solutions. Eastman manufactures both traditional Conventional Tubular , tubular batteries and lithium‑ion battery packs, including those for light electric vehicles. Its vertically integrated setup allows for better quality control and innovation speed. 
 
Distribution & service network: 
Eastman has developed a large distributor network and service partner ecosystem across India with over 1400+ distributors and 3300+ service partners covering more than 350 districts. This ensures reliable after‑sales service and product availability across the country. 


Consistent growth & market leadership 
 
Strong growth trajectory: 
Eastman has shown remarkable expansion. The company grew nearly fivefold in the last five years, driven by its dominance in last‑mile e‑mobility battery business and adoption of renewable + storage solutions. 
 
Product credibility and certification: 
Eastman’s product catalog includes lithium‑ion batteries designed for energy storage (solar + inverter + storage systems) rated for high cycle life. These products are designed and manufactured in India, aligning with the Make in India initiative. 
 
Brand reputation & workplace culture: 
Eastman is recognized not only for its products but also as an employer of choice. It has earned certifications for workplace excellence and manufacturing quality, underscoring a culture of innovation and reliability. 

Future vision & customers Trust  
 
Commitment to energy transition: 
Eastman’s roadmap emphasizes integrated energy generation, storage batteries, and power electronics. This holistic offering helps customers adopt clean energy with confidence. 
 
Innovation & continuous improvement: 
With dedicated R&D resources, Eastman continues to upgrade battery chemistries (lithium‑ion,Conventional Tubular replacements) and design solutions that fit solar + backup + mobility use cases. This innovation ensures that customers receive future‑ready products. 
 
Reliability & customer reassurance: 
With decades of market presence, large manufacturing scale, widespread service network, and product warranty / high‑quality standards, customers feel assured when they choose the brand. Eastman is among the most trusted names in inverter batteries, solar batteries, and e‑mobility energy solutions. 
 

Trust built over decades 

When consumers or businesses look for reliable energy solutions, they want more than just specifications they want consistency, reputation, and long‑term assurance. Over more than twenty years, Eastman Auto & Power has built just that: a reputation for delivering quality, innovation, and dependable service. 
 
From powering homes with backup inverters, enabling rooftop solar storage, to driving e‑rickshaw fleets with advanced lithium battery packs, the brand has demonstrated reliability across applications. Today, Eastman stands as one of India’s most trusted energy brands, leading the charge in solar, battery, and e‑mobility solutions.
