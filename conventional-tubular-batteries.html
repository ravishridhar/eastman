Conventional Tubular Batteries

Eastman is one of the largest Indian manufacturers of conventional tubular batteries, specializing in inverter, solar, and e-rickshaw batteries. Our manufacturing line is set up with advanced German technology, enabling us to produce up to 5.5 million units per year across three factories to meet the ever-growing demand of our channel partners.

We are recognized as the industry leader in manufacturing capacity for low-speed E3W batteries and have established ourselves as India's largest conventional tubular batteries manufacturer.
