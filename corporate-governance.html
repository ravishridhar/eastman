Board of Directors

Mr. Satpal Kumar Arora

Independent Director

Satpal Kumar Arora is an Independent Director on the Board of our Company. He holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce (honours) and a master’s degree in commerce from University of Delhi. He is an associate member of the Indian Institute of Bankers and the Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India and a registered insolvency professional from Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India. He has more than 30 years of experience, including his experience in the compliance sector. He was previously associated with IFCI Limited and Alchemist Asset Reconstruction Company Limited. He has also served as a member with CARE Ratings Limited and as an advisor with The National Small Industries Corporation Limited.

Board of Directors

Mr. Rajat Diwaker

Independent Director

Rajat Diwaker is an Independent Director on the Board of our Company. He holds a bachelor’s degree in engineering from Nagpur University and a post graduate diploma in business management from Institute of Management Technology, Ghaziabad. He has approximately 20 years of experience in the field of sales. He is currently associated with iD Fresh Food (India) Private Limited as a chief executive officer – India and was previously associated with Pidilite Industries Limited, SBI Cards and Payment Services Private Limited, Marico Bangladesh Limited, PepsiCo India Holdings Private Limited, NourishCo Beverages Limited and The Walt Disney Company (India) Private Limited. Further, he was awarded “Tata Gluco Plus” award for outstanding contributions to PepsiCo in 2016.

Mr. Sanjeev Gupta

Independent Director

Sanjeev Gupta is an Independent Director on the Board of our Company. He holds a bachelor's degree in commerce from Arya College, Ludhiana, Panjab University. He is a fellow member of the ICAI. He has over 37 years of experience in the field of corporate taxation, statutory and internal audits of corporate entities and corporate consultancy. He is currently serving as a proprietor of Sanjeev Bimla & Associates since September 5, 1988 and is currently a director on the board of Eastman Hong Kong Private Limited.

Board of Directors

Ms. Manjusha Bhatnagar

Independent Director

Manjusha Bhatnagar is an Independent Director on the Board of our Company. She holds a bachelor’s degree in science (chemistry, botany and zoology) from Bhopal University and a master’s degree in business administration from Rajasthan University. She is admitted as a member of the Institute of Directors. She has more than 37 years of experience, including experience in entrepreneurial development. She was previously associated with Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Madhya Pradesh Consultancy Organisation Limited, Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited, and was on the board of Balmer Lawrie Van Leer Limited. She has also been awarded the ‘Professional Woman of 2008’ award by the Forum for Women in Public Sector (WIPS), MRPL, Mangalore and the Mother Teresa Excellence Award-2011 by the International Integrity Peace and Friendship Society.
