Corporate History

Two and a half decades of building for the future.

From a single company with a clear vision, we have grown into a group of businesses spanning energy storage, power electronics, lithium technology, solar, and global markets.

2000

Eastman Auto & Power Incorporated

2008

Established / Commissioned our first manufacturing facility located at Solan Unit 1

2016

Incorporated AMPS Middle East FZ LLC

2018

Commissioned our second manufacturing facility located at Solan Unit 2

2020

Incorporated Eastman New Energy Private Limited to manufacture lithium batteries

2021

Achieved a turnover of more than ₹9,500.00 million in Fiscal 2021

2022

Incorporated Eastman Middle East FZ CO

2024

Achieved a turnover of more than ₹35,000.00 million in Fiscal 2024

2024

Launched 'ERB I-Connect' mobile application for monitoring of batteries for e-rickshaws

2024

Launched 'EastmanOne' mobile application for monitoring of Solar Inverters

2024

Launched app 'PowerONE’ for Dealer/ Distributor/ Salesforce Management

2025

Launched 'AI based Voicebot' for registering the complaints and troubleshooting

2025

Achieved cumulative production and sales exceeding 10.00 million e-rickshaws batteries since inception

2025

Established Jhundpur Unit and Bahalgarh Unit with a focus on achieving backward integration in Power Electronics

2025

Achieved 11 giga watt capacity for manufacturing of our energy product portfolio

2025

Introduced 'Eastman's LithTec Combo', offering compact, efficient, and eco-friendly backup power solutions for Indian households

2025

Established the Sonipat Unit through our Subsidiary, Eastman Green Technologies Private Limited, for manufacturing of crystalline silicon terrestrial photovoltaic (PV) modules

2026

Achieved a turnover of more than ₹57000.00 million in Fiscal 2026
