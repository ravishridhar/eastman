Key Managerial Personnel

Mr. Shekhar Singal

Managing Director

Shekhar Singal is the Managing Director on the Board of our Company. He has been associated with our Company since January 3, 2006. He holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce from Panjab University, and a post graduate diploma in business management from Institute of Management Technology, Ghaziabad. He has over 19 years of experience in the electronics and battery manufacturing sector. He currently manages strategy, financial performance and business operations of our Company. He is also involved in overseeing the governance standards of our Company.

Key Managerial Personnel

Mr. Ashok Kumar Jain

Whole-time Director and Chief Financial Officer

Ashok Kumar Jain is a Whole-time Director and Chief Financial Officer on the Board of our Company. He has been associated with our Company since August 12, 2024. He holds a bachelor's degree in commerce from St. John’s College, Agra, Agra University and a master’s degree in commerce from Agra University. He is a fellow member of the ICAI, the ICSI and the Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India. He is also a professional individual member of the All India Management Association. He has more than 37 years of experience in the field of finance and accounts. He was previously associated with Luminous Power Technologies Private Limited, Utility Forms Private Limited and Global Autotech Limited. He currently oversees financial strategies, cost optimisation, process automation and financial planning of our Company.

Mr. Goutam Kumar

Whole-time Director

Goutam Kumar is the Whole-time Director on the Board of our Company. He has been associated with our Company since April 1, 2023. He has passed the examination for the bachelor's degree in arts from Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag and holds a post graduate diploma in business management with specialisation in rural marketing from the Indian Institute of Rural Management. Key Managerial Personnel

Mr. Rajesh Jindal

Chief Executive Officer

Rajesh Jindal is the Chief Executive Officer of Eastman Auto & Power Ltd. He brings over 34 years of industry experience across Sales, Marketing, and Product Management.

Before joining Eastman Auto & Power Ltd., he spent 24 years at Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Ltd., where he most recently served as President & Chief Marketing Officer.

Mr. Jindal holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh, and a Master’s degree in Business Administration from Panjab University.

Key Managerial Personnel

Mr. Rahul Nitin Sinnarkar

Company Secretary and Compliance Officer

Rahul Nitin Sinnarkar is the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of our Company. He has been associated with our Company since February 10, 2025, and was subsequently appointed as the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer of our Company on March 17, 2025. He manages corporate and secretarial compliance of our Company. He holds a bachelor’s degree in commerce from the Kelkar Education Trust’s Vinayak Ganesh Vaze College of Arts, Science and Commerce, University of Mumbai, and holds an advanced diploma in business administration from Prin. L. N. Welingkar Institute of Management Development & Research. Further, he is an associate member company secretary of the ICSI. He has also passed the professional competence examination held by the ICAI. He has more than 10 years of experience in the field of finance, compliance and secretarial. Prior to joining our Company, he was associated with Syrma SGS Technology Limited, Carbay Services Private Limited (an entity of CarDekho Group), Sahara International Airport Private Limited and L&T Hydrocarbon Engineering Limited.
